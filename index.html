Selected Publications

My research spans embodied intelligence, trustworthy agents, and security. Selected work is highlighted.

Uni-VLaT: Whole-Body Tactile Adaptation of VLA Policies for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation

Shutong Liu · First author

Under review at IEEE ICRA 2027

Adapting pretrained VLA policies with whole-body tactile sensing and tactile-anchored multimodal prediction for contact-rich humanoid control. Achieves 75% average success across five real-robot tasks.

From Frozen Pose to Live Dance: Stochastic Browser Fingerprinting for Robust Risk-Based Authentication

Shutong Liu · First author

Under review at USENIX Security 2027

Stochastic browser fingerprinting introduces randomized rendering challenges to defend risk-based authentication against replay attacks while preserving stable device identification.

Mitigating Agentic Deception via Evaluation-Side Partially Observable Games

Shutong Liu · Second author

In preparation for ICLR 2027

An agent–monitor game with a fixed verifier mitigates emergent deception. Two-timescale reinforcement learning strengthens oversight while preserving task performance, including under weak-to-strong supervision.

Safety and Security of Satellite Communications: A Comprehensive Review of Radio-Frequency and Free-Space Optical Systems

Weizhe Liu, Shujiang Wu, Shutong Liu, Qiang Wei, Yunhong Wang

Submitted to Security and Safety

A review of safety and security in radio-frequency and free-space optical satellite communications, organizing threats, countermeasures, and open research directions.
